Why farms choose TLREPEAL

Red mites feed on birds at night, cutting egg production and welfare. TLREPEAL attacks them from inside the bird and on the nest at the same time.

  • Natural solution against avian red mites
  • Blend of plant-based essential oils
  • Can be applied externally or added to drinking water
  • Induces taste avoidance, smell repellency, contact and vapour toxicity
  • Easy to use and administer
  • Low probability of developing resistance
  • Makes the working environment safe for employees

Questions about TLREPEAL

Is TLREPEAL safe for farm workers?

Yes. It is non-harmful to birds and humans and makes the working environment safer.

How do I rotate water and spray?

Use 150 ml per 1,000 L in drinking water one week and spray nests at 300 ml per 1,000 L the next.

Will mites become resistant?

The probability is low, because TLREPEAL acts through four different modes at once.
