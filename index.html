The challenge

Antibiotic resistance has reached the farm gate

For decades antibiotics were used to grow birds faster and prevent disease. Overuse in human and veterinary medicine created resistant bacteria and left residues in meat and eggs.

  • Regulators are restricting antibiotic growth promoters
  • Export buyers test for antibiotic residues
  • Consumers ask for antibiotic-free meat and eggs
The Remevia answer

Nature already has the molecules. We make them work.

Plants defend themselves with essential oils and oleoresins. We identify these molecules, extract them gently and blend them into precise, easy-to-dose formulations.

  • Feed, drinking-water and spray formats
  • Multi-target blends with low resistance risk
  • Safe for birds, animals, workers and consumers

From plant to poultry in four steps

Every Remevia formulation follows the same path before it reaches a farm.

  1. Identify

    Screen plant compounds with proven antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory and gut-health effects.

  2. Extract

    Recover essential oils, oleoresins and carotenoids while keeping the active molecules intact.

  3. Formulate

    Blend compounds that act on several targets at once, so pathogens and pests struggle to adapt.

  4. Validate

    Test in the lab and on live flocks, then standardise every batch before it ships.
