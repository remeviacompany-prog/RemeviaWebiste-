Why farms choose CURTECT-CRD

Respiratory stress from dust, ammonia, heat or vaccination slows growth and raises losses. CURTECT-CRD clears airways naturally through the drinking water.

  • Bronchodilator, expectorant, mucolytic and decongestant action
  • Anti-inflammatory
  • Prevents viral and bacterial penetration through the mucosal lining
  • Prevents microbial attachment to the trachea
  • Antimicrobial, antioxidant and immune-stimulating
  • Easy to use and administer

Easier breathing

How it works

Six actions work together on the airway, so the flock breathes easier and microbes find it harder to settle.

Expectorant: clears mucus
Mucolytic: thins mucus

Anti-inflammatory
Blocks microbial attachment to the trachea

Questions about CURTECT-CRD

Is CURTECT-CRD an antibiotic?

No. It is a blend of plant essential oils and terpenes and leaves no antibiotic residues.

How fast does it work?

Flocks typically show relief from respiratory distress within 24–48 hours.

Can I use it with vaccination programmes?

Yes. Start 48 hours after vaccination at 200 ml per 1,000 L, twice a day for 4 days.
