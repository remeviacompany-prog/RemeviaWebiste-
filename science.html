Our R&D process

Four steps take a plant molecule from the field to a proven product.

  1. Identify

    Screen botanical compounds with known antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory and repellent properties.

  2. Extract

    Recover pure essential oils and oleoresins without damaging the active molecules.

  3. Formulate for synergy

    Combine compounds that hit a problem from several angles, lowering the chance of resistance.

  4. Validate

    In-vitro lab work and in-vivo flock trials back every claim with data.

One molecule can be beaten. A combination is much harder.

Bacteria and pests adapt to a single mode of action. Blends that act on several targets at once give them far fewer ways out.
