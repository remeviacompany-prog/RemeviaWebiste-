Formats that fit your routine

In feed

TLTREAT and TLEGG mix into premixes and complete feed at 1–2 kg per tonne. Supplied in 50 kg lined laminated bags.

In drinking water

CURTECT-CRD and TLREPEAL dissolve in the water line at 150–200 ml per 1,000 L. Supplied in 5 kg and 20 kg bottles.

As a spray

TLREPEAL treats nests and housing. TL HEALER goes directly on wounds and tick bites.
