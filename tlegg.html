Why farms choose TLEGG

Buyers pay more for golden yolks. TLEGG delivers that colour from plants, so you can cut maize or synthetic pigments without losing it.

  • Supplement that boosts egg yolk colour
  • Contains carotenoid extracts from marigold flowers

  • Maintains yellow yolk colour with less maize
  • Easy to use and administer

Golden yolks

Feed flexibility

Keeps colour stable when maize in the ration is reduced.

How it works

Fat-soluble carotenoids from marigold are absorbed with dietary fat and deposited in the yolk, building colour over the feeding period.

Marigold carotenoids (lutein)
Lipophilic: absorbed with dietary fat

Does TLEGG contain synthetic pigments?

No. Colour comes from marigold carotenoids and spice extracts.

Can I reduce maize when using TLEGG?

Yes. TLEGG helps maintain the yellow yolk colour even with a lower quantity of maize.

What is the inclusion rate?

1 kg of TLEGG per 1,000 kg of layer feed.
