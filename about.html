Our story

Remevia began in Irinjalakuda, Kerala, when our founders saw how heavily poultry farming had come to rely on antibiotics, and how much risk that created for people and the planet.

They believed the answer already existed in nature. Kerala's spices and medicinal plants have protected people and animals for centuries. Today we combine that heritage with modern extraction and formulation to give farmers a reliable natural alternative.
