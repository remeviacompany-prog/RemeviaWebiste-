Why farms choose TL HEALER

Open wounds attract flies and infection and slow recovery. TL HEALER cleans, protects and repels flies in one natural spray.

How to apply

  1. Clean the area

    Remove dirt and debris from the wound if possible.

  2. Spray or apply

    Cover the wound, tick bite or injury with TL HEALER.

  3. Repeat

    Reapply as needed until the skin has healed.
