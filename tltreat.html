Questions about TLTREAT

Can TLTREAT fully replace antibiotic growth promoters?

Yes. TLTREAT is designed as a natural alternative to AGPs for broilers and layers at 1–2 kg per tonne of feed.

When should layers receive TLTREAT?

From week 60 to week 110 at 2 kg per tonne of feed, to support an extended laying cycle and better returns.

Does TLTREAT help with coccidiosis?

Regular use helps prevent coccidiosis as part of a good management programme.
