Common questions

Do you offer free trials?

Yes. Choose "Request a free trial" above and our technical team will plan a trial for your flock or feed mill.

Which products go in drinking water?

CURTECT-CRD and TLREPEAL are water soluble. TLTREAT and TLEGG are mixed into feed. TL HEALER is applied to the skin.

What pack sizes are available?

Liquids come in 5 kg and 20 kg HMHDPE bottles. Powders come in 50 kg PP woven laminated bags with an HMHDPE liner.
